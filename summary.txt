Russia and Ukraine ended US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi with no ceasefire but agreed the war's biggest prisoner exchange, over 1,000 a side, negotiators said; US envoys head to Moscow on Thursday to meet Vladimir Putin (Reuters, AP). NATO scrambled jets after drones strayed into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia (Just Security). President Trump said talks with Iran were 'very good' and the war would be 'over quickly', pausing a Hormuz escort operation, as reports said a near deal would have Iran forgo nuclear weapons and halt enrichment for at least 12 years; Tehran said it had yet to reply and set seven conditions (Reuters, Axios). Iran's Guards said two supertankers were disabled by explosions in Hormuz on Wednesday night (Reuters). US prosecutors charged three men over Iran-backed plots including one at RAF Fairford, and Treasury Secretary Bessent moved to a military base after threats (NYT). France accused Iran's Guards of hacking about 700 supermarket websites (Reuters). House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth (Reuters). AP called Mississippi's Senate race for Democrat Scott Colom, who beat Cindy Hyde-Smith by about 4,700 votes to become the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction; Democrats then held 55 Senate seats, with only Alaska uncalled, and 255 House seats to Republicans' 168, with 12 House races uncalled (AP, NPR). UN chief Guterres told COP31 leaders in Antalya the world was on a path to destruction without fast climate action (UN News). Anthropic filed to list at a reported $1.4tn valuation (Reuters). The Wall Street Journal reported Nvidia's board was preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang; its shares fell over 8% after hours (WSJ, CNBC). US inflation ran hot for a third straight month, Fed's Christopher Waller raised the prospect of a half-point rate rise, and the S&P 500 fell 0.4% (Reuters). A UK court struck down Trinidad and Tobago's ban on same-sex relations.
