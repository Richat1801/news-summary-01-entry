Russia and Ukraine ended two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi with no ceasefire but agreed a large prisoner exchange, the biggest of the war, envoy Jared Kushner said; US envoys head to Moscow on Thursday to meet Vladimir Putin (Reuters, AP). NATO scrambled jets after drones strayed into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia (Just Security). President Trump said talks with Iran were 'very good' and the war would be 'over quickly', pausing a Hormuz escort operation, as reports said a deal was near under which Iran would forgo nuclear weapons and halt enrichment for at least 12 years; Tehran said it had yet to reply and set seven conditions (Reuters, Axios). US prosecutors charged three men over Iran-backed plots including one at RAF Fairford, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base after threats (NYT). France accused Iran's Revolutionary Guards of hacking about 700 supermarket websites (Reuters). In Washington, House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth (Reuters). A week after the midterms, AP counts gave Democrats 254 House seats to Republicans' 163 and at least 54 Senate seats, with 16 races still uncalled. UN chief Antonio Guterres told COP31 leaders in Antalya the world was on a path to destruction without fast climate action (UN News). Anthropic filed to list at a reported $1.4tn valuation while Nvidia fell amid a board probe of its chief (Reuters). US inflation ran hot for a third straight month, Fed governor Christopher Waller raised the prospect of a half-point rate rise, and the S&P 500 fell 0.4% (Reuters). A UK court struck down Trinidad and Tobago's ban on same-sex relations.
