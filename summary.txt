Russia and Ukraine held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, their first direct negotiations in nine months; the opening sessions were called constructive but brought no breakthrough (BBC, Reuters). US prosecutors charged three men with plotting Iran-backed attacks on RAF Fairford in Britain and on Jewish communities, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has moved onto a military base after Iranian threats against him, the New York Times reported. France summoned Iran's ambassador and accused the Revolutionary Guards' cyber unit of hacking about 700 supermarket websites, spoiling food (Reuters). President Trump said he would hold off on new strikes on Iran after appeals from Gulf allies, even as US forces kept building up in the region (Reuters). In Washington, House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth (Reuters). A week after the midterm elections, AP counts gave Democrats 254 House seats to Republicans' 163 and at least 54 Senate seats, with 16 races still uncalled. Anthropic filed for a stock-market listing as Reuters reported it was eyeing a $1.4 trillion valuation, while Nvidia shares fell again amid a board investigation into CEO Jensen Huang's alleged links to a suspected Chinese intelligence operative (The Information, Reuters). US inflation ran hot for a third straight month, Fed governor Christopher Waller raised the prospect of a half-point rate rise, and the S&P 500 fell 0.4% (Reuters). World leaders met in Antalya, Türkiye, for the leaders' days of the COP31 climate summit, hosted by Türkiye with Australia leading the negotiations (UN News). Bulgaria held a national day of mourning for the 12 people killed on Tuesday when 86 vehicles piled up in fog on the Trakia motorway (BTA, AP). A UK court struck down Trinidad and Tobago's ban on same-sex relations. NASA's Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth, farther than any human-made object has travelled (New Scientist).
